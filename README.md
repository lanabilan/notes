# ReadTheKey

Practice reading piano notes — free, no ads, no paywall.

Open the app, see a note on the staff, tap the matching key on an on-screen piano. That’s the whole loop.

**[Practice now →](https://readthekey.readthekey.workers.dev)**

## Why it exists

Most note-reading apps interrupt practice with ads or a paywall. ReadTheKey does not. You can start a round immediately, with no account.

## How practice works

Each round is 10 notes on treble clef (natural notes from C4 to G5):

1. A note appears on the staff.
2. You tap the matching key.
3. You get instant correct / wrong feedback.
4. On a miss, you can reveal the right key and note name — or skip ahead.
5. After 10 notes, you see accuracy and average response time, then practice again or switch sets.

Two sets:

- **Random** — notes from the range, in mixed order
- **Stepwise** — neighbouring notes, moving up and down the staff

Optional pitch playback lets you hear the correct note after each tap.

Built for the phone: staff, piano, and score on one screen, with large tap targets.

## Accounts

You don’t need one. Guests can complete full rounds.

Sign in if you want to keep your last score and current set. Progress is stored only for signed-in users.
